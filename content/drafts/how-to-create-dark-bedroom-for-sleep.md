## Why This Matters

Many hygiene problems come from missed steps rather than lack of effort. For this topic, the main risk is poor sleep quality from light exposure, inconsistent timing, and stimulation. A structured routine helps reduce that risk by keeping actions in a stable order. Core principle: Keep bedroom temperature around 65-68°F (18-20°C) and use blackout curtains or eye mask. Dark, cool environments promote restful sleep.
