How to wash feet between toes properly may look simple, but consistent technique matters when you want reliable results. This guide gives you a repeatable routine you can follow quickly without skipping key hygiene checks. Use it as a practical checklist, not a one-time fix.

## Why This Matters

Many hygiene problems come from missed steps rather than lack of effort. For this topic, the main risk is sweat-related irritation, odor, and skin barrier imbalance. A structured routine helps reduce that risk by keeping actions in a stable order. Core principle: Feet accumulate moisture and bacteria. Wash between toes during every shower and dry completely to prevent athlete's foot and fungal infections.
