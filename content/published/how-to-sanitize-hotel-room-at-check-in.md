Even clean-looking hotel rooms harbor germs on high-touch surfaces. Taking a few minutes to sanitize upon arrival can help you stay healthy during your trip.

## Why This Is Important

Hotel housekeeping works hard, but they have limited time. With check-out at 11 AM and check-in at 3 PM, staff may only have 15-20 minutes per room. Studies show TV remotes, door handles, and light switches can be significantly germier than toilet seats. Taking control of these surfaces gives you peace of mind and reduces illness risk.

![Wiping a hotel TV remote with a disinfectant wipe](/images/tips/how-to-sanitize-hotel-room-at-check-in-1.png)

## How to Do It

### Step 1: Pack the Right Supplies

Bring travel-sized disinfectant wipes that specifically kill viruses, not just cleaning wipes that remove visible dirt. Look for products labeled as disinfectants. Keep them easily accessible in your carry-on.

### Step 2: Wipe High-Touch Surfaces

Focus on areas you'll touch frequently. Start with the TV remote—one of the germiest items in any hotel room. Then wipe down all light switches, door handles, and the phone. Don't forget nightstands, lamp switches, and the refrigerator handle if your room has a mini-fridge.

![Disinfectant wipes, spray bottle, and hand sanitizer in a travel bag](/images/tips/how-to-sanitize-hotel-room-at-check-in-2.png)

### Step 3: Sanitize Bathroom Touch Points

In the bathroom, wipe down the toilet handle or flush button, faucet handles, and bathroom counter. If there's a hair dryer, sanitize the handle and buttons before use. Pay attention to any switches or knobs on the vanity area.

![Wiping bathroom faucet handles with a disinfectant wipe](/images/tips/how-to-sanitize-hotel-room-at-check-in-3.png)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Using baby wipes or cleaning wipes instead of disinfectants
- Forgetting to wipe the toilet handle and bathroom faucet
- Wiping only visible dirt without using proper disinfecting products
- Touching your face before washing your hands after sanitizing

## Pro Tips

> "The average hotel door handle has almost 1000 more germs than a toilet seat at home."

- Do this quick 2-minute sweep before unpacking your luggage
- Place items you don't need to touch frequently on sanitized surfaces
- Use hand sanitizer after touching public areas like elevator buttons
- Consider using a ziplock bag for the TV remote if you want extra protection
